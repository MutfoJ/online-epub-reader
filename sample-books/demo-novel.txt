Chapter 1 The Door

The rain had been falling for hours before Mara opened the letter.

It was the sort of rain that made windows look like old glass and streets look like memory. By the time the envelope was in her hands, the apartment had gone dim enough that the city lights were brighter than the room.

She read the first line twice.

Chapter 2 The Stairs

The key was exactly where the letter promised it would be, taped beneath the third stair with the care of someone who expected doubt.

Mara stood in the hallway listening to the building breathe around her. Pipes clicked. Someone coughed behind a distant wall. She put the key in her pocket anyway.

Chapter 3 The Room Above

The room smelled of paper, dust, and the sweet trace of cedar.

Shelves climbed all the way to the ceiling. In the middle sat a table under a single lamp, and on the table lay a book with no title on the cover.
